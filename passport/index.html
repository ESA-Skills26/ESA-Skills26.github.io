GRADUATE JOURNEY

From registration to benefit

  1. Registration

    Basic data, the exact name for the certificate and contact consent.

  2. Requirements check

    Attendance, assessment, tests and program requirements are reviewed.

  3. Certificate issued

    A certificate with its own serial number and a QR code for verification.

  4. Membership created

    A Graduate ID is generated and the certificate is linked to it.

  5. Activation message

    You receive your Graduate ID and your card link.

  6. Explore benefits

    Services, offers and events available to graduates.

  7. Use benefits

    Your membership is checked and each use is recorded.

  8. Profile grows

    Every new certificate is added to the same ID.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

How do I get my Graduate ID?

It is sent to you in your activation message once your certificate is issued. If you did not receive it, message us on WhatsApp.

Who can join?

Every trainee who meets the requirements of an ESA completion certificate, in any program.

What does a public lookup show?

Only your name, tier, membership status and your ESA certificates. Your phone number and email are never shown.

My name or a certificate is wrong. What do I do?

Use the “Request a correction” button on your card; it sends us your ID on WhatsApp so we can fix it.
